Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game One Fleet! Idle adventure (the "Game") handles your information. The Game is published by Sobrasada Studio ("we", "us"), based in France. If you have any question, contact us at sobrasadastudio@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account. The Game has no login.
  • Your progress (XP, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • To improve the Game, we measure how it is played (progress, play time, ads shown, purchases) with GameAnalytics, using a random player ID, not your identity.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising partners (Unity and Google) collect some device data to show and measure ads. When you first open the Game, you choose whether ads can be personalized, and you can change this anytime in Settings.
  • You can buy optional packs in the Game's shop (gold, scrolls and a special ship). Payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your game progress (XP, upgrades, tutorial status), your advertising choice and the gold, scrolls and ships you received from purchases on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us (the gameplay statistics described in section 5 are separate). You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads (rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus, and full-screen ads at some moments in the Game) through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliates ("Unity"), and the ad network Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC and its affiliates ("Google").

To deliver ads, Unity and Google may collect the following information directly from your device:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone, Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking when iOS asks) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location (country) derived from it;
  • device information: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, time zone, mobile carrier, connection type, free storage and battery level;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them (views, clicks, completions).

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, measure ad performance, and pay us for the ads shown. If you consent, it can also be used to show ads that match your interests (personalized ads).

We don't receive this data in a way that identifies you. Unity and Google process it under their own privacy policies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services (Google Privacy Policy).

4. In-app purchases

The Game's shop offers optional packs of gold and scrolls, and a special ship (the shiny wreck) that is sometimes offered during play. Buying is never required to play.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone). We never see your name, email or payment details. The store tells the Game only which product you bought, with a purchase receipt. Your payment is processed under the Google Privacy Policy or Apple's Privacy Policy.

The Game uses Unity's in-app purchasing library (Unity IAP) to talk to the store. This library may send technical information to Unity (such as device model, operating system, the product bought and whether the purchase succeeded) to operate and improve the service, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

What you get from a purchase (gold, scrolls or a ship) is added to your game progress, which is saved only on your device. These items are used up in the Game and cannot be restored: if you uninstall the Game or clear its data, they are lost together with your progress. For refunds, see Google Play's or Apple's refund policy, or contact us.

5. Game analytics

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it (for example, which waves are too hard, how long play sessions last, and whether our ads and marketing work), the Game uses GameAnalytics, a game analytics service operated by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark). GameAnalytics processes this data on our behalf.

The Game sends GameAnalytics the following information:

  • a random player ID created by the Game when you first open it (it is not your name or your account);
  • device information: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, connection type, and your country, which GameAnalytics derives from your IP address;
  • how you play: when you open and close the Game and how long you play; your progress in the Game, such as the waves you reach and the battles you win or lose;
  • the ads shown to you (type of ad, and the estimated revenue the ad earned us, as reported by our ad partner);
  • if you buy something in the shop: the product, its price and currency (never your payment details);
  • technical errors that occur in the Game, so we can fix them;
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you accepted personalized ads (see section 6). Otherwise only the random player ID is used.

We use this information only in aggregated reports (for example, how many players reach wave 10) and don't use it to identify you. We don't sell it. GameAnalytics handles it under its privacy notice and data retention policy.

6. Your choice about personalized ads

The first time you open the Game, we ask whether you accept personalized ads. The Game shows ads either way:

  • Accept: ads can be based on your interests, and your advertising ID is also used for game analytics (section 5).
  • No thanks: ads are not personalized. We also ask our partners not to sell or share your personal information.

On iPhone, iOS then asks whether the Game may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with our ad partners.

You can change your choice anytime in the Game: Settings → Personalized ads (ON / OFF). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

8. Sharing and international transfers

We don't share your information with anyone other than Unity (ads and in-app purchase library), GameAnalytics (game analytics, on our behalf) and Google Play or Apple (payments), as described above. GameAnalytics may transfer data outside the European Economic Area with appropriate safeguards (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses), as explained in its privacy notice. Unity may share ad-related data with advertisers and measurement partners, and may transfer it outside your country (including to the United States) with appropriate safeguards, as explained in Unity's privacy policy.

9. Data retention

Data saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We keep no copy. Advertising data is kept by Unity according to its privacy policy. Game analytics data is kept by GameAnalytics according to its data retention policy; we only look at it as aggregated reports.

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under 15 in France). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw your consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

  • For data saved in the Game: uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • For game analytics data: email us. We ask GameAnalytics to delete the data linked to your device.
  • For advertising data: use the in-game setting above, reset your advertising ID, or contact Unity as described in its privacy policy.
  • To have your data deleted, email us at sobrasadastudio@gmail.com.

For any other request, email us at sobrasadastudio@gmail.com. We'll answer within one month.

If you are in France or elsewhere in the EU, you can also file a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the French Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (www.cnil.fr).

12. Security

We don't run servers that store your information. Your progress stays on your device, protected by your phone's security. Unity and GameAnalytics protect the data they process as described in their privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if we add new features. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be announced in the Game or on its store page.
